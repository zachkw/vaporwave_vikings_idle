# Art

This folder will hold the art direction and asset specs. The art is made by the project's artist; the style is not decided yet and will be filled in later. Anything drawn in code (such as the proof's parallax shapes) is a throwaway placeholder.

## Principles already agreed

- **One art style, multiplied.** Make one style of assets by hand first, then use AI to multiply it into the full set.
- **Volume over polish.** Lots of variety matters more than finish.
- **Readable when busy.** Colour shifts and stacked dimensions must not hide the Viking, enemies or coins.
- **Neon Norse.** Vikings through a vaporwave lens: bold silhouettes, mythic weapons, runic motifs, saturated colour.
- Hidden or small gear (undershirt, underwear, socks, rings, necklace, earring) may only need an icon.
- Artefacts are one piece of art each; weapon effects reuse each other's visuals.

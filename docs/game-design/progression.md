# Progression

How the player moves through the world, and how ascension resets and rebuilds them.

## Areas, levels and bosses

- Each biome is played as a run of surface levels, each about 5 minutes long and ending in a boss.
- The boss fight is automatic and checks damage, health and defence. Win and the Viking advances to the next level or biome and earns a badge. Lose and a brand-new level is built for the same biome and level number, and the Viking starts at its beginning; gold and gear are kept, so he farms until strong enough.
- If the Viking is far too weak, the boss kills him at once with a signature move.
- Defeating a boss advances automatically; there is no confirmation screen.
- Open: how many levels per biome, and what raises a biome's level within a world (W1).

### Badges

The HUD shows a badge for each biome beaten in the current world. A new World+ level starts a fresh set; earlier clears do not count.

## World+

- When every biome in the sequence is beaten, World+ starts automatically: the same biomes again, harder and richer.
- World+ keeps gold, gear and unlocks. It is not a reset and awards no ascension points.
- Within a world, biomes also get gently harder as their level rises, and pay more gold. World level and biome level are tracked separately.
- Eventually the gear the player can afford no longer keeps up. Growth slows, and that is the cue to ascend.

## Portals

- After a full clear, portals can appear that send the Viking to a random other biome. The player chooses whether to enter, not where it goes.
- Portals let the player revisit biomes for ingredients, materials and missed courses.
- A talent can add a portal at the start of a biome: 20, 40, 60, 80, then 100 percent chance across five levels.
- A portal jump does not pay distance gold for the skipped ground.
- Open: how often ordinary portals appear and where the Viking arrives.

## Ascension

Ascending is optional. The player chooses when.

- **What resets.** Everything, by default: gold, gear levels, artefacts, ingredient and pickup unlocks, course completions, world progress. Something survives only if the player owns a talent that makes it permanent.
- **What stays.** Ascension level, unspent points, talents bought, lifetime gold.
- **Points.** Points come from lifetime gold earned. Each point needs a bigger slice of gold than the last, forever, across ascensions. Spending gold on gear does not slow it.
- **Level.** The ascension level equals points earned; one point, one level.
- **Pending and banked.** The ascension screen previews points earned since the last ascension. Ascending banks them. Banked points can be spent on talents at any time, without ascending again.
- **First offer.** June proposed offering the first ascension on beating World+1, after a few sessions (S1).

### Talent web

Points are spent across a large web of talents. Proposed shape: many cheap stat talents, plus a few expensive gateway talents that each open a system. Gateway candidates so far:

- One per Village building: garden, gathering huts, hunting lodge, tanning benches, apothecary stations
- Keep artefacts between ascensions
- Make other unlock kinds permanent (ingredients, pickups, courses)
- Auto-consume rules for ingredients
- Always show which pits are caves
- Higher letter tiers of assault course
- Biome-start portal chance (five levels)
- Later: automation of away play, such as bosses or unlocks

A simple stat talent to start with, from June: +5 percent all gold per level.

Open: the full web (S2).

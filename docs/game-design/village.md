# Village

The Village is a separate upgrade layer that plays like a small village idle game: buildings automate production and the player taps to collect, in a light Clash of Clans style. Everything it makes feeds gold generation.

## How it opens

- Ascension talents unlock Village buildings, one gateway talent each.
- Claude's reading: so there is no Village in the very first run. Open (V4): whether buildings, seeds and stock persist through later ascensions.

## Buildings

| Building | What it does | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Garden | Grows ingredients from seeds | A steady supply of dimension ingredients |
| Gathering huts | Send villagers out to gather | Mushrooms and other natural ingredients |
| Hunting lodge | Send villagers out to hunt | Not defined (V5) |
| Tanning benches | Process skins | Leather; demon hide becomes demon armour |
| Apothecary stations | Mix ingredients | Balms, medicines and remedies (effects open, D7; June idea: balms make the Viking's skin glow and raise damage and other stats) |

Tanning benches, apothecary stations and similar crafting buildings are the workshops. More buildings will follow.

## Garden and seeds

1. The player gets the garden first.
2. Achievements then appear for each plant: "eat this plant N times to unlock 1 seed".
3. Planting a seed grows ingredients. Exact yields and growth times are open (D6).
4. Garden ingredients are mixed with something to make a smoother effect that lasts the whole level (tentative, D1).
5. End state: the player eats an ingredient every time they enter its biome, automatically, because seeing that dimension always means more gold.

Open: how the garden is acquired and how many eats per seed (D3).

## Materials and villagers

- Workshops take pelts, leathers and demon hide from kills.
- Materials are used to make money, make villagers and the Village stronger, and upgrade artefacts. They are never spent on core gear.
- Open: how materials become money (V1), what a stronger villager or Village does (V2, V3), and whether villagers are managed or just timers (V6).

## Away from the game

The first version of away play earns gold only. Village production while the app is closed needs its own rules before it is built.
